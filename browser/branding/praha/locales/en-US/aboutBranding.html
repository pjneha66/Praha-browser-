Privacy controls

Praha starts with strict tracking protection. Here’s where to find your privacy controls.

Cookies and trackers
Open Settings → Privacy & Security to review tracking protection and choose which site data to keep.
Encrypted DNS
In Privacy & Security, find DNS over HTTPS to change your resolver or protection level.
Ad blocking
Open Add-ons and themes → Extensions to manage uBlock Origin and its permissions.
